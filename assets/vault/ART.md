# The Sunken Vault artwork

The eight transparent sprites were generated together for this project with OpenAI image generation, then cropped and scaled for a 64-pixel grid. The diver and crab use the same collision rules as the original agents. The floor and wall fill one cell. The pressure plate and lever switches sit beneath the characters. Separate closed and open hatch images show door state. The renderer supplies movement interpolation, character idle motion, labels, selection and status feedback.
